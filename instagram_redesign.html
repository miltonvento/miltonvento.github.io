“No More CRAPtions”: Redesigning Instagram for Deaf and Hard‑of‑Hearing Communities

Project information

  • Category: Human and Computer Interaction
  • Project timeline: May 2025
  • Medium Blog: Click here to read my Medium blog on this project

  • In this HCI project focused on Accessibility, I explored how to make Instagram more accessible for users with auditory disabilities, including those who are Deaf or Hard of hearing.
  • Recommended Changes :
  • - Prompt creators to review/edit captions before posting
  • - Auto-generate lyrics for music in static posts
  • - Offer a “Speech Enhancement” tool to boost voice tracks
  • - Implement an Accessibility setting to permanently disable auto-play audio across app sessions.
  • - Enable searchable, downloadable transcripts in the video player
