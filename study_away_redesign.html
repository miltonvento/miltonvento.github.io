Reimagining Study Away: A Mobile-First Experience for Williams Seniors

Project information

  • Category: Human and Computer Interaction
  • Project timeline: May 2025
  • Medium Blog: Click here to read my Medium blog on this project

  • In this HCI project I designed a mobile interface that improves my school’s Global Education & Study Away website, with a specific focus on graduating seniors.
  • Design Stages and improvements:
  • - Created paper prototypes and conducted usability tests
  • - Created a moodboard for visual design inspiration, using other university websites as reference
  • - Created high fidelity Figma prototypes based on initial paper sketches, user feedback, and moodboard
  • - The redesigned site included visual and functional improvements such as clearer button styles, a global navigation system, and larger header image with foreground text. The new design features were in alignment with Gestalt principles
